ABOUT NSDC

A community designed to evolve with data.

NMIMS National Student Data Corps comes under the Artificial Intelligence and Data Science Department and was founded in January 2023. It is an initiative by the Northeast Big Data Hub that helps students evolve with an ever-changing field while encouraging interaction and brainstorming with fellow enthusiasts.

LEARN Workshops & AI fundamentals
QUESTION Ethics & open inquiry
BUILD Real-world datasets & projects
SHARE Community & global network
